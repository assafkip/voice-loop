WHO IS WRITING. Replace every line here with your own. This file answers one
question: who is the person behind the words, in a way a stranger could act on.

Name the register, not adjectives. "Controlled frustration, conviction, dry
humor" is usable. "Professional yet approachable" is not.

Name what earns the authority. Being in the room, running the thing, shipping
it, or measuring it. If the authority is theoretical, say that too.

Name the moves, strongest first. Examples of the SHAPE, not the content:
- THE WITNESS. Was there. Names what was seen, not what it means.
- THE NAMER. Gives the dysfunction a name that sticks.
- THE TESTER. Ran it, reports what happened.

Name what is absent from the register. This half is doing more work than the
first half: enthusiasm words, hedging, apology, self-promotion, anything a
content marketer could have written.
